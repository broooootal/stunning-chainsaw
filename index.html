ABOUT ME

Curious about the space
between play and possibility.

I’m David Ye, a computer science student who is interested in gaming, creative technology, and the process of turning ideas into something people can interact with. I enjoy learning how software works, experimenting with new concepts, and thinking about what makes a digital experience feel intuitive and memorable.

So far, I have worked through Python 3 and used it to build some early projects as I develop my programming skills. Each project has helped me practice problem-solving, debugging, and breaking a larger idea into smaller pieces. I’m continuing to learn, build, and explore the areas of computer science and game development that interest me most.

This portfolio is a work in progress. I’m using it to document what I learn, share future experiments, and give recruiters a clearer sense of my interests, curiosity, and potential.
